00Brief & assumptions
WHAT YOU ASKED FOR

Six predicted numbers, drawn from a range you can edit, laid out in space, answering automatically. Every control below is live — edit the range and the prediction re-forms.

HOW IT PICKS

The God's Eye lab's own math: a 4-octave value-noise FBM field is laid across the number pool, and the six highest peaks are the draw. Real computation, not a fake chart.

"IN SPACE"

I read that two ways and built both: a flat field strip across the range (1a), and a true orbital star-field where every candidate is a point of light (1b).

OPEN QUESTIONS

Desktop or phone first? Should past draws be kept and scored? And the honest one: independent draws can't be forecast, so do you want the UI to admit that or stay in-character?
